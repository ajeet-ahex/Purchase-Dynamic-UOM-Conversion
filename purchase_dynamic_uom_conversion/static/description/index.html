The Problem Odoo Doesn't Solve Out of the Box

Procurement teams in manufacturing, distribution, and wholesale run into this constantly. Your supplier quotes in their unit of measure — kilograms, litres, pallets, reels, metric tonnes. Your warehouse receives and counts in a completely different unit — boxes, units, grams, pieces, metres.

Odoo's standard purchase module handles basic UoM conversion within the same category (e.g., kg to g), but it can't handle cross-category conversion — buying in a completely different unit than you stock (like kg from a vendor, boxes on your shelf). It also doesn't give you a purchase-specific unit with its own pricing logic, vendor-facing quantity display, and automatic two-way price synchronisation. That's the gap this module fills.

The Vendor Unit of Measure Converter for Purchase module closes that gap. As a lightweight third-party UoM conversion app, it adds a dedicated Purchase UoM field per product, a configurable conversion ratio, automatic bidirectional pricing, and supplier-ready reports — entirely within Odoo's purchasing layer, with nothing touching your stock records.
✕ Without this module

Manually calculate quantities per PO line, maintain a conversion spreadsheet, risk data entry errors on every order, send confusing purchase orders to vendors who quote in different units, and lose visibility on true per-unit landed cost.

✓ With this module

Configure a conversion ratio once per product. Enter quantities in the supplier's unit. Odoo auto-calculates stock quantities, syncs pricing both ways in real time, and prints vendor-ready reports with their unit and product code on every PO.

Quick summary: If you've ever asked "what's the cost per box when my supplier prices by kg?" or "why doesn't the stock quantity match what I ordered?" or "can Odoo show both the vendor's unit and my stock unit on the same PO line?" — this module is exactly what you need.

Pricing Formula — Transparent & Auditable

Unit Price (Base) = Price per Purchase Unit × Conversion Ratio

The formula is applied on every purchase order line in real time. Change the purchase price → base price recalculates. Change the base price → purchase price recalculates. Change the ratio → both prices recalculate simultaneously. The logic is entirely transparent and can be validated by any Odoo developer from the 231-line codebase.

Product Configuration — Step by Step

One-time setup per product. Once configured, every subsequent purchase order for that product converts automatically. No further action needed for your procurement team.

1

Open the product form

Navigate to Purchase → Products → Products and open the product you want to configure for dual-unit purchasing. Any product that is purchased from a supplier with different units is a candidate.

2

Locate the Conversion Details section

This module adds a dedicated Conversion Details section to the product form. All conversion-specific fields live here — separate from the standard product tabs so they don't interfere with existing workflows.

3

Set your Purchase UoM

Select the supplier's unit of measure (e.g., kg, litre, carton, pallet). This must be a valid Odoo unit — if it doesn't exist yet, create it under Configuration → Units of Measure first.

4

Enter the Conversion Ratio

Enter how many Purchase UoM units equal one Base UoM unit. The value must be greater than zero. Use decimals (e.g., 0.001) when you purchase in a unit larger than your stock unit.

Example: if 1 bag (base) = 2.5 kg (purchase), enter 2.5. If 1 gram (base) = 0.001 kg (purchase), enter 0.001.
5

Set either price field — the other calculates automatically

Enter either the Price per Purchase Unit (the supplier's quoted price) or the Price per Base Unit (your internal cost). The other field calculates automatically using the conversion ratio. Both stay in sync from this point forward.

6

Add the supplier and vendor product code

In the Supplier Info tab, add the supplier and fill in their Vendor Product Code. This code appears on RFQ and PO PDF reports in the supplier-facing columns — formatted exactly as vendors expect.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I buy this app? Odoo already has UoM conversion built in?

Odoo's built-in conversion only works within fixed categories, like kg to g. It can't handle a vendor selling in cases or tonnes while you stock in units or kg. Our module adds a configurable purchase-unit ratio with automatic price sync, so you never do the math by hand.

How is this different from a "secondary unit of measure" app for purchase?

Most secondary UoM apps just display a second quantity or price alongside the original — you still enter and reconcile both manually. This module goes further: set the conversion ratio once per product, and the purchase unit and base unit prices stay in sync automatically, in both directions, on every order line.

Will this affect my warehouse stock quantities and valuations?

No. The conversion logic applies only inside the purchasing environment. Your warehouse inventory continues to use the Base UoM for all stock counts, valuations, and reorder calculations. Nothing changes for your warehouse or inventory team — the module is purely additive to the purchase flow.

I updated the product configuration but my existing PO isn't updating. Why?

Existing purchase order lines store the conversion values at the time of creation — this is standard Odoo behaviour to protect confirmed orders from unexpected changes. To apply updated ratios, re-select the product on the order line or update the quantity field to trigger a recalculation with the new settings.

The vendor product code isn't showing on my report. How do I fix it?

Navigate to the product's Supplier Info tab, add the specific supplier, and fill in their Vendor Product Code field. The report pulls this value per supplier — so it must be configured for each vendor separately. One product can have different vendor codes for different suppliers and the correct one will appear on each PO.
